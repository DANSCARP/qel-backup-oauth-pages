# QEL Backup

**QEL Backup** is a private backup utility used by its owner to store encrypted backups of the Quant Engine Lab research project in Google Drive.

## Purpose

The application uses rclone and Restic to transfer encrypted backup data to the owner's Google Drive account and support recovery when necessary.

## Data access

The application is intended exclusively for personal use. It does not offer public registration, sell user information, or provide third-party access to research data.
