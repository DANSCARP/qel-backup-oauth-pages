# Privacy Policy — QEL Backup

**Last updated:** November 1, 2026

## 1. Purpose

QEL Backup is a personal backup application used by its owner to securely store and recover encrypted backups of the Quant Engine Lab research project using Google Drive, rclone, and Restic.

## 2. Information accessed

The application uses Google OAuth authorization to access Google Drive for backup and recovery operations.

Access is limited to the permissions granted by the Google account owner.

## 3. Data storage and security

Backup contents are encrypted by Restic before being transferred to Google Drive.

Authentication credentials are stored locally and are not intentionally published or shared with third parties.

## 4. Data sharing

QEL Backup does not sell personal information or provide backup data to advertisers.

Google Drive processes stored data according to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Data retention and deletion

Backup data is retained until the account owner chooses to remove it.

The account owner may revoke the application's Google access through their Google Account security settings.

## 6. Intended users

The application is intended solely for the personal use of its owner and does not offer public user registration.
